Transcript Deletion After the Summary

Transcript Deletion After the Summary

Section titled “Transcript Deletion After the Summary”

Some organisations need a meeting’s transcript to exist only while it is being used: to power the meeting assistant during the meeting and to write the summary at its end. LMA can delete each meeting’s transcript permanently once the meeting is summarized, either immediately or 15, 30 or 60 minutes later, and keep the summary.

This is a stronger guarantee than a short retention period. The Days to keep transcript segments setting gives each segment a DynamoDB time to live, and DynamoDB removes expired items in the background, typically within a few days of their expiry rather than at it. Deletion after the summary does not rely on that: it deletes the transcript at the minute it is due.

Turning it on

Section titled “Turning it on”

The choice is the Retention › Delete the transcript after the summary setting on the Configuration page, which an Admin can change at any time. It ships set to Off. The choices are:

ChoiceEffect
Off (the default)The transcript is kept for Days to keep transcript segments, as before.
ImmediatelyThe transcript is deleted as soon as the summary is stored.
15, 30 or 60 minutes after the summaryThe transcript is deleted that long after the summary is stored, which leaves time to read or download it.

A saved change applies to the next meeting that ends: the deletion reads the setting as saved each time a meeting ends or is summarized. A meeting that has already ended keeps the deletion time it was given, and a meeting that ended while the setting was off keeps its transcript for the days set above.

The Regulated – consent required, transcript not retained configuration preset sets this to 15 minutes after the summary, together with opt-in consent, no audio recording and redaction, and Regulated – notify with opt-out, transcript not retained sets the same with notify consent; Standard and Internal meetings – notify participants set it to Off. See Configuration presets, which also lists the retention stack parameters a preset cannot set, and Meeting Consent.

When the transcript is deleted

Section titled “When the transcript is deleted”
  • When the summary is stored, the delay starts counting. With Immediately, the transcript is deleted at that point.
  • If no summary has been stored 60 minutes after the meeting ends (the summary step failed, or is still running), the delay counts from then instead. The transcript is deleted whether or not a summary was ever written. Until then an Admin can regenerate a failed summary from the meeting page; once the transcript is deleted, Regenerate is refused.
  • On a deployment with no end-of-meeting summary (EndOfCallTranscriptSummary set to LAMBDA with no EndOfCallLambdaHookFunctionArn), the delay counts from the end of the meeting.
  • After a deletion made immediately, the meeting is checked once more ten minutes later. That removes anything written for the meeting after the deletion, such as a meeting assistant answer that was still being written or a segment the speech engine corrected after the meeting ended.

A scheduled deletion runs at its time even if the setting has since been turned off. Participants may have been told the transcript would be deleted, and turning the setting off does not undo that promise for meetings that have already ended.

What is deleted and what is kept

Section titled “What is deleted and what is kept”

Deleted, for the meeting:

  • every turn of the transcript, partial or final, from the meeting’s transcript records in DynamoDB, including the meeting chat messages a Virtual Participant added to it;
  • every meeting assistant answer stored with the transcript;
  • the Issues detected field, which quotes the transcript, on a meeting recorded by a version of LMA before 0.4.0 that still carries it;
  • the transcript file in the recordings bucket that the summary step writes for the transcript knowledge base, its metadata file, and, for an uploaded meeting, the Amazon Transcribe output file. Every version of each file is deleted, not only the current one;
  • those files’ entries in the transcript knowledge base, when the deployment has one.

Kept:

  • the summary, on the meeting and in the transcript knowledge base, so the meeting can still be found and asked about. Whatever the summary quotes from the transcript, or from the meeting chat (a link a participant shared, for example), stays in it;
  • the meeting record: its title, times, participants, agenda, speaker names and sentiment;
  • the audio and video recordings, which follow AudioRecordingExpirationInDays. Turn Record meeting audio off on the Configuration page if the audio must not be kept either;
  • shared-screen key frames and the text read from them, which follow Days to keep transcript segments in full;
  • the consent record, which is the evidence that participants were told.

What users see

Section titled “What users see”

While deletion is pending, the meeting page shows above the transcript when it will be deleted, so anyone who needs a copy can download it first. Once it is deleted, the page says when, and the transcript download is no longer offered. A meeting page that is open when the meeting ends shows the deletion time a few seconds after the summary appears. It shows that the transcript has been deleted the next time it is loaded.

The Details tab’s Capture and processing section says the same under Transcript retention: when the transcript is to be deleted or was deleted, that the Delete the transcript after the summary setting did it, how many transcript segments and files were removed, and that the summary, the recordings and the meeting record are kept. For a meeting whose transcript is not deleted after the summary, it says that the transcript is kept for the days set to keep transcript segments.

After the deletion:

  • Regenerate on the summary is refused, because a new summary would be written from no transcript and replace the one that was kept. Editing the summary by hand still works.
  • The meeting assistant has no transcript of the meeting to read. Questions about past meetings are answered from their summaries.
  • The meeting is counted the same way in the meetings list and in usage and cost records.

How it is carried out

Section titled “How it is carried out”

The call event processor tells the transcript deletion function when each meeting ends and when its summary is stored. The function either deletes the transcript there and then, or creates a one-time EventBridge Scheduler schedule that invokes it at the minute the deletion is due. The schedule deletes itself once it has run. A deletion is only ever moved earlier, never later, so a repeated end-of-meeting event cannot postpone it. The time each meeting is due is written onto the meeting record, as TranscriptDeletionScheduledFor.

When the deletion has run, the meeting record also carries TranscriptDeletedAt, TranscriptSegmentsDeleted and TranscriptFilesDeleted. Together these are a record that the deletion happened, readable through getCall.

Every step is safe to repeat. A deletion that fails is retried: by Lambda for an asynchronous invocation, and by EventBridge Scheduler for a schedule, for up to 24 hours. A deletion that still has not completed is kept on a dead-letter queue, and the transcript-deletion-dead-letter-queue-depth alarm notifies the operational alerts topic (see Monitoring & Alerting). The alarm’s description says how to run the deletion again. The usual cause is a recordings bucket the stack did not create, with S3 Object Lock or a bucket policy that does not allow the deletion.

As a backstop, while deletion is on each transcript segment and meeting assistant answer is also written with a DynamoDB time to live of at most two days. Key frames keep their usual expiry. If a deletion never ran at all, DynamoDB still removes the segments, on its own schedule.

Copies outside the transcript store

Section titled “Copies outside the transcript store”

The deletion removes the transcript from where LMA stores it. Copies also pass through these places, each with its own lifetime:

WhereHow longWhat to set
The Kinesis call data stream24 hours, the shortest Kinesis allowsNothing; records age out on their own
The meetings table’s DynamoDB stream24 hoursNothing; records age out on their own
The meetings table’s point-in-time recovery35 days: deleted items can be restored from a backup until thenPoint-in-time recovery is a safety protection and stays on. If your policy forbids restorable copies, raise it with whoever owns the deployment’s backup policy
Lambda and container logsCloudWatchLogsExpirationInDaysKeep LogLevel at INFO (the default) or above. At DEBUG, functions log the transcript text they handle
The GraphQL API’s log group, /aws/appsync/apis/<api id>CloudWatchLogsExpirationInDaysKeep AppSyncFieldLogLevel at ERROR (the default) or NONE. At DEBUG or ALL, the API logs each request’s content, transcript segments included
Amazon Bedrock model invocation loggingAs configured for the accountOff by default. If the account has it on, every prompt is logged, including summary prompts that contain the whole transcript
The summaryKept with the meetingSummary prompts can be written not to quote the transcript; see Transcript Summarization

Limitations

Section titled “Limitations”
  • The setting applies to the whole deployment. There is no per-meeting choice.
  • The delay is counted to the minute: EventBridge Scheduler runs a one-time schedule within the minute it is set for.
  • A meeting that ended before the setting was turned on keeps its transcript for Days to keep transcript segments.
  • With a recordings bucket the stack did not create, the transcript files are only deleted if the bucket allows it.
  • A transcript knowledge base the stack did not create is asked to remove the files’ entries. If it does not ingest from the recordings bucket, there is nothing to remove.
