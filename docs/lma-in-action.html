Capture

It joins the meeting for you, on any platform

Paste the meeting link and a Virtual Participant joins as a guest, now or at a scheduled time. It introduces itself in the meeting chat, listens, watches the shared screen, and leaves when the meeting ends. Prefer not to add a participant? Capture from your own browser, the Chrome extension, the Desktop Capture App for macOS and Windows, or upload a recording.

Anyone in the meeting can talk to it in the chat: LMA help, LMA pause, LMA ask …. Two people can each bring their own LMA and address it by name.

Why it matters

One assistant for every customer's and every team's platform of choice, with nothing to install for the other participants.

Meeting sources

Hybrid meetings

Everyone in the conference room gets their own name

When several people share one room microphone, most transcripts collapse them into "Conference Room". LMA tells the voices apart and names each one from what they say, such as their own introductions, and shows the quote it used beside each name. Merge a stray voice in one click, or mark a seat as one person or several.

Why it matters

Action items and decisions are attributed to the right person, even when half the meeting is in the room.

Speakers and diarization

Live transcript

A live transcript, in the language you read best

Every line appears seconds after it is spoken, labelled with its speaker, and can be translated into your language as it arrives. Search it, filter it by speaker, and play the recording from any line. Choose Amazon Transcribe, or an on-demand open-model speech engine that runs in your account.

Why it matters

Multilingual teams follow the same conversation in real time, and nobody has to wait for the recording.

During the meeting

Ask anything, while it still matters

The meeting assistant is an agent with tools, not a chatbot over a transcript. It reads the meeting so far, then reaches for whatever it needs to answer: past meetings, your documents, the web, or your own systems.

Meeting assistant

"What is still open, and who owns it?"

Ask in plain language, or press a quick action: summarize so far, list actions, find topics, or flag action items with no owner or due date. Answers stream in a few seconds and cite what was said.

Then go further: "search the web for current best practices on what we just discussed". The assistant runs the search, reads the results and answers in the context of your meeting.

Why it matters

Gaps are caught while everyone is still in the room to close them, not in a follow-up email a day later.

In the meeting chat

Anyone in the meeting can ask, right from the chat

Type LMA what was just discussed? in Zoom, Teams or Meet chat, and the answer is posted back to the meeting chat for everyone, and recorded in the transcript. Latecomers catch up without interrupting, and people without an LMA login still benefit.

With the voice assistant turned on, participants can say its wake phrase and hear the answer spoken, and an optional avatar gives LMA a face on camera.

Why it matters

The assistant serves the whole meeting, not only the person who invited it.

Voice assistant

Extensible

Connect it to your own systems with MCP

Give the assistant new tools by adding Model Context Protocol servers: install one from the public MCP registry, such as Salesforce, or point it at your own endpoint, with OAuth or an API key. Built-in tools already search this meeting's transcript, agenda and slides, your past meetings, your documents in a knowledge base, and the web.

Ask "pull up this account's open opportunities" during a sales call, or "is there a ticket for this bug?" in a stand-up.

Why it matters

Answers come from your own data, in the moment, without anyone leaving the call to look it up.

MCP servers

Integrate

Bring your meetings into your AI agents

LMA is also an MCP server. Connect Amazon Quick Suite, Claude Desktop or your own agents, and they can search your meetings, read transcripts and summaries, and even send a Virtual Participant to a meeting, now or on a schedule.

Interactive clients sign in with OAuth; scheduled and headless agents use a personal API key. Each user sees only their own and shared meetings.

Why it matters

Meeting knowledge flows into the tools people already use, instead of sitting in another silo.

After the meeting

A record you can act on

Summaries, actions, agenda coverage and the slides that were shown, ready as soon as the meeting ends, and searchable across every meeting you have had.

Summary and agenda

Did we cover what we came for?

Paste the invitation and LMA reads the agenda. Afterwards the summary shows which agenda items were covered, which were only touched on, and which were missed, alongside the key points, decisions and action items. Summaries are Markdown you can edit, regenerate safely, download or share.

Why it matters

Follow-ups start from what was actually agreed, and nothing on the agenda silently falls off.

Meeting agenda

The shared screen

The slides are searchable too

LMA keeps key frames of whatever was shared, reads the text on them, and adds a shared-screen section to the summary. Search for a figure that was on a slide and jump straight to it, and the moment in the meeting when it was shown.

Why it matters

What was shown is captured as faithfully as what was said.

Across meetings

Ask across all your meetings

"Across our meetings, what has been decided about the launch, and what is still open?" The Meetings Query Tool answers from every meeting you can see, and lists the meetings it drew on.

Why it matters

A project's history is one question away, even when it was spread across a dozen calls.

Trust

Consent and retention you can prove

Built for organisations that have to answer "who agreed to this, and where is the data now?"

Informed consent

Everyone knows, and you can show it

Choose Off, Notify or Opt-in for each meeting. LMA posts its notice in the meeting, and any objection stops it. Every meeting keeps a consent record, with the notice that was given, the responses, and screenshots as evidence.

Why it matters

Recording policy becomes something you can demonstrate meeting by meeting, not just a line in a handbook.

Meeting consent

Retention

Keep only what you need, for as long as you need it

Set how many days to keep meeting records and transcripts, or delete each transcript as soon as its summary is written, so only the summary remains. Deleting a meeting deletes its recordings too.

Why it matters

You decide the balance between a useful record and the smallest possible footprint, and it applies automatically.

Transcript deletion after the summary

Transparent cost

See what every meeting cost

Each meeting shows how it was captured and an itemised estimate of what it cost: transcription, model tokens for each feature, the voice assistant and the Virtual Participant's compute, at dated list prices. Default models are chosen and tuned for quality at a low cost per meeting, and you can switch to any model you prefer.

Why it matters

No per-seat licence: you pay only for the AWS services each meeting uses, and you can see exactly where it goes.
