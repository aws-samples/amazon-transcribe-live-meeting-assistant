# LMA in Action

A one-page, illustrated tour of what Live Meeting Assistant does during and after a meeting, and what sets it apart from a note taker: it joins any meeting platform, names the voices in a conference room, answers questions while the meeting is still going (from the transcript, your documents, the web, or your own systems through MCP), brings your meetings into AI agents such as Amazon Quick, and keeps consent and retention under your control, in your own AWS account.

**▶ [Open LMA in Action](https://aws-samples.github.io/amazon-transcribe-live-meeting-assistant/lma-in-action.html)**

Source: [`docs/lma-in-action.html`](https://github.com/aws-samples/amazon-transcribe-live-meeting-assistant/blob/main/docs/lma-in-action.html). The screenshots are in [`images/lma-in-action/`](https://github.com/aws-samples/amazon-transcribe-live-meeting-assistant/tree/main/images/lma-in-action), taken from a test deployment with synthetic meetings and voices.
